The Classic Choice

Beijing + Shanghai

Imperial China meets futuristic China — the perfect way to fall in love with the country on your first trip.

Why is this the smartest pick for a first trip?

  • Direct Air France flights — Paris to Beijing outbound, Shanghai to Paris return: no layovers, no hassle. A perfect "open jaw" itinerary
  • Maximum contrast — Beijing is millennia of history (Forbidden City, Great Wall, hutongs), Shanghai is jaw-dropping skylines and modernity. Two faces of China in one trip
  • High-speed train — The Beijing → Shanghai bullet train (4h30 at 350 km/h) is an experience in itself and saves you a travel day
  • Perfect tourist infrastructure — Metro, English signage, international hotels. Ideal for your first encounter with China
  • Iconic cuisine — Peking duck in Beijing, xiaolongbao in Shanghai: China's most celebrated dishes right at your fingertips
  • The perfect intro — If China wins you over (and it will), you'll come back for Zhangjiajie and Chongqing with the right appetite for adventure

The Urban Adventure

Beijing + Chongqing

From imperial grandeur to the most cyberpunk city in China — a journey between history and vertigo.

Why this combination?

  • Best of both worlds — The essentials of Beijing (Great Wall, Forbidden City) + China's most authentic and futuristic city in Chongqing
  • Chongqing is unique in the world — Built on mountains, with roads passing through buildings, metro trains entering apartment blocks, and legendary hotpot
  • Less touristy, more authentic — Very few Westerners in Chongqing: you'll immerse yourselves in real Chinese life
  • Internal flight via Air France/partners — Air France operates through SkyTeam partners (China Eastern/China Southern) on domestic routes

The Dream Trip

Chongqing + Zhangjiajie

The China you don't expect: Avatar mountains, a cyberpunk city, and a Beijing stopover so you won't have regrets.

Why consider this even for a first trip?

  • The Avatar mountains are real — The sandstone pillars of Zhangjiajie inspired Pandora in the film. A landscape that takes your breath away
  • Chongqing is your dream — And dreams shouldn't be postponed. The craziest vertical city in the world deserves to be seen
  • Smart Beijing stopover — With a layover in Beijing (even just one night), you'll see the Forbidden City and come home with zero regrets
  • A trip unlike any other — While everyone does Beijing+Shanghai, you'll discover deep China. A real adventure, not a tourist package
  • Beijing+Shanghai can wait — Those cities are always there. The Avatar mountains and Chongqing are far more unique experiences
